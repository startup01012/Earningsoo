## Impact on Features

### Relative Returns (`relative_return_*`)
- Computed as: `stock_return - niftybees_return`
- ETF tracking error introduces small noise (~basis points)
- Dividend reinvestment in ETF NAV vs price index creates drift
- **Acceptable for research** - noise is small relative to stock returns

### Abnormal Returns (`abnormal_return_*`)
- Computed as: `stock_return - benchmark_return`
- Same considerations as relative returns
- **Acceptable for research**

### Targets (`benchmark_return_*_after`, `return_*_after`)
- Benchmark returns use NIFTYBEES raw prices
- Subject to same corporate action issues as stock prices
- **Documented limitation** (see price_integrity_audit.txt)
